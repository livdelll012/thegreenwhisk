A fusion table, set in matcha green

The Green Whisk started with a Syrian kitchen, a Puerto Rican culinary tradition, and a small matcha habit picked up while studying in New Haven, Connecticut. None of those worlds usually share a table, so this collection imagines what happens when they do.

Syrian baking invites aromatic semolina, orange blossom, rose, and pistachio. Puerto Rican cooking brings coconut, coffee, and the slow-simmered comfort of coquito and arroz con leche. Matcha ties them together in a quiet, earthy, thread. Each recipe is a deliberate pairing to discover where these flavors can discover agreement with each other.
